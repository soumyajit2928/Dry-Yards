Past the Accord line

Inside Lantern Row the Accord of Quiet Hands keeps the peace: no guns, fists only. Two kilometres east the Accord ends at a painted line on the asphalt, and past it lie the Dry Yards, a salvage town nobody has governed since the Grid Collapse.

The Yards still hold what the Row needs: copper in the warehouse walls, filters in the old clinic, batteries buried under the plaza. Crews cross the line with scavenged rifles and shotguns and fight for each block until one crew has enough kills to make the rest go home.

Every crew starts at its corner. Buildings have doors on some sides and solid walls on others. Tree canopies hide you from anyone further than a few steps away until you fire. Medkits sit in the plaza and the clinics; armor plates sit in the corner buildings. Both come back after a while.

Every player's position is always on the minimap, and arrows at the screen edge point to anyone off-screen. Hold M for the full map. In the street, enemies only show when you have line of sight. Walls and trees stop bullets. Standing under a tree canopy hides you from enemies further than a few steps away until you fire. Armor soaks 60% of incoming damage. You respawn at your crew's corner 3 seconds after going down.

On touch screens, drag the left half to move and the right half to aim and fire.
